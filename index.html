Our Philosophy

Where Every Cup Tells an Origin Story.

At Costa, we believe great coffee is never rushed. We partner directly with high-altitude family farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala—paying well above fair-trade minimums to honor the hands that harvest every cherry.

Back in our neighborhood roastery, we roast in small batches to unlock natural notes of toasted hazelnut, dark cacao, and stone fruit. Pair your pour-over with our sourdough croissants in a sunlit space built for slow mornings and meaningful conversation.
